  7. 12:30to 13:00
    6 LEIR · South Hall Bldg 150 Now

    Low Energy Ion Ring, with Aaron

    LEIR takes pulses of lead ions from Linac 3 and squeezes them into short, dense bunches for the LHC.

    15 min · 1.1 km Aaron leads the way west: Route Albert Picot, Route Babbage, then down Route WU to Building 150. Route Bakker, the shorter way, is closed for road works.

    Leave R1 by 12:15: the walk takes 15 minutes.
